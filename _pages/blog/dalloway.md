# Mrs Dalloway

A short review of a short story taking place in a short period of time that somehow took me way longer to read through.

There are many memorable characters that seems to represent many aspects of british society (maybe Woolf is trying to say something here). The author does a very good job of bringing attention to seemingly mundane and everyday things, who would have thought getting flowers or catching a bus would be so interesting! The internal thoughts of the characters reflecting on their past makes me feel old though...
